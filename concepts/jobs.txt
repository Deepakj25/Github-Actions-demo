Job is the smallest independent schedulable unit in a GitHub Actions workflow. It consists of one or more sequential steps and executes on its own runner.

Workflow
   │
   ├── Job
   │     ├── Step
   │     ├── Step
   │     └── Step
   │
   └── Job
         ├── Step
         └── Step

Workflow → contains Jobs
Job → contains Steps
Step → executes an Action (uses) or Script (run)

Execution Behavior
Parallel (Default)

Sequential
Use needs to create dependencies.

test:
  needs: build

Multiple Dependencies
  deploy:
  needs:
    - build
    - test

Key Takeaway
Jobs are parallel by default.
Steps are always sequential.
Every job gets a fresh runner.
Steps in the same job share the same runner and workspace.
needs is what creates dependencies between jobs.
