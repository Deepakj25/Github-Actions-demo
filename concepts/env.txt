Environment Variables (env)

env is used to define user-created environment variables that can be reused throughout a workflow.
You own the value. GitHub does not modify it.

env can be defined at three levels:
Workflow
   │
   ├── Job
   │      └── Step

Workflow Level
Available to all jobs and steps.

Job Level
Available only within that job.
Overrides workflow-level variables.

Step Level
Available only for that step.
Overrides job/workflow variables.
Exists only for the duration of that step.

Variable Precedence
Step env
    ↑
Job env
    ↑
Workflow env

Rule: The most specific scope wins.
