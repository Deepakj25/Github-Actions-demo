Data Flow

Within a Job
────────────
Step 1
   │
   ▼
Step 2

Use:
✅ GITHUB_ENV


Across Jobs
───────────
Job 1
   │
   ▼
Job 2

Use:
✅ GITHUB_OUTPUT + Job Outputs

GITHUB_OUTPUT creates step outputs.
Stored in workflow metadata
Every producing step must have an id.
Access outputs using
Outputs are available only after the step finishes. (from next step)

${{ steps.step_id.outputs.output_name }}

Now comes the missing piece:
Step Outputs are only available within the same job.
How do we make them available to another job?
That's where Job Outputs come in.

Step Output
      │
      ▼
Job Output
      │
      ▼
Another Job
